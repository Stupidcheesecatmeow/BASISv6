WELCOME TO BASIS!

Welcome to BASIS (Bagac Attendance and Scholar Information System)! We're glad to have you as a Representative of the Iskolar ng Bagac community. This platform is designed to help you manage scholar-related information, monitor attendance, keep track of activities, and stay updated on important announcements. Through BASIS, you can help ensure that information is organized, activities are properly monitored, and scholars remain informed about their responsibilities.

Being a Representative is an opportunity to lead, assist, and support your fellow scholars. Your role is important in promoting responsibility, encouraging participation, and helping ensure that scholarship activities run smoothly. We hope BASIS will be a valuable tool in fulfilling your duties and making a positive impact on the Iskolar ng Bagac community.

Before proceeding, please take a moment to read the guidelines on the next page to understand your responsibilities and how to use the system properly.

GUIDELINES!

1. Complete your profile. Make sure all required personal information is filled out correctly and kept up to date.

2. Create announcements. Post important announcements, reminders, and updates to keep scholars in your designated barangay informed.

3. Create activities. Create and manage activities for scholars, ensuring that the necessary details and requirements are provided clearly.

4. Respond to feedback and concerns. Regularly check and respond to feedback, questions, and concerns submitted by scholars from your designated barangay. Provide helpful responses and assistance whenever possible.

5. Stay active and responsible. Check BASIS regularly, keep information updated, and fulfill your duties to help maintain an organized and supportive scholarship community.

Remember: As a Representative, your leadership, communication, and commitment help keep scholars informed, engaged, and supported in their scholarship journey!

INACTIVE SCHOLAR ACCOUNTS

If a scholar is no longer part of the Iskolar ng Bagac program, their BASIS account will be marked as inactive, and they will no longer be able to log in or access the system.

This may apply to scholars who have graduated or have officially left the program.

If you believe your account was mistakenly deactivated and you are still an eligible scholar, please contact the President of Iskolar ng Bagac directly to report the issue. Provide your name and relevant details so your scholar status can be verified. Account reactivation will only be considered if the deactivation was made in error and your eligibility is confirmed. Once approved, your account access may be restored by the authorized personnel.
