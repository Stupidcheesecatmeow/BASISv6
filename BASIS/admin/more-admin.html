FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

1. What is BASIS?

BASIS is a web-based system designed to help Iskolars, Representatives, and Administrators manage and monitor scholarship activities, attendance records, scorecards, and announcements in one platform.

2. Who can use the system?

Only authorized Iskolars, Representatives, and Administrators of the Iskolar ng Bagac Program can access the system.

3. How can I log in to my account?

Open the BASIS website and enter your authorized login credentials. If you cannot log in, check your credentials or contact an Administrator for assistance.

4. How can I record my attendance?

You can record your attendance by having your activity QR code scanned by an authorized Representative or Administrator. If necessary, you may also submit proof of attendance through the system.

5. What should I do if I cannot attend an activity?

You may submit proof of absence through the system, following the program's requirements. Your submission will be reviewed by an authorized Representative or Administrator.

6. Will my submitted proof automatically be approved?

No. All submitted proofs of attendance and absence must be reviewed and verified by an authorized Representative or Administrator before approval.

7. How can I check my attendance records and scorecard?

Log in to your account and open the Scorecard section to view your available attendance records and scholarship-related performance information.

8. What should I do if my attendance record or scorecard is incorrect?

Contact an authorized Representative or Administrator and provide the details of the incorrect record so it can be reviewed.

9. Where can I view announcements and activity schedules?

You can check the Dashboard, Announcements, or Activity section of the system for updates, upcoming activities, schedules, and other scholarship-related information.

10. Can I access BASIS without an internet connection?

No. BASIS requires a stable internet connection to access its features. Offline access is not supported.

11. Can I use my scorecard as an official scholarship document?

No. The scorecard is intended for attendance monitoring and reference purposes only. It cannot be used as an official document for scholarship validation, certification, or other formal transactions.

12. Who should I contact if I experience a problem with the system?

Contact an authorized Representative or Administrator for assistance with login problems, attendance records, proof submissions, scorecards, or other system-related concerns.

13. Can I access BASIS using my mobile phone?

No. BASIS is currently designed for desktop and laptop computers only. It does not support a mobile-friendly view, so some features and layouts may not display properly on mobile phones. For the best user experience, access the system using a desktop or laptop computer.

1. Acceptance of Terms

All users must read and understand these Terms and Conditions before using the system. Users who do not agree with these terms should refrain from using BASIS and contact an authorized Administrator for assistance.

2. User Accounts and Access

Users must use their own authorized accounts and provide accurate and updated information in their profiles. Login credentials must be kept confidential and must not be shared with others. Users may only access the features and information permitted by their assigned roles. Any suspected unauthorized access or account misuse must be reported to an Administrator.

3. Use of the Dashboard

Users may access their respective dashboards to view scholarship information, announcements, notifications, activities, attendance records, and other features available to their assigned roles. Users are responsible for checking their dashboards regularly for important updates and must not attempt to access restricted information or functions.

4. Scorecard and Attendance Records

Iskolars may view their own scorecards and attendance records through BASIS. Activities should only appear in a scholar's scorecard when supported by the scholar's own verified attendance record, according to the system's attendance rules. Users must report incorrect or incomplete records to an authorized Representative or Administrator. Unauthorized modification, manipulation, or falsification of attendance records and scorecards is prohibited.

5. Activity Participation and Attendance Monitoring

Iskolars must follow the attendance requirements and participation guidelines established for scholarship activities. (1) Attendance may be recorded using the system's QR code scanning feature or other authorized attendance procedures. Users must not use another person's QR code, submit false attendance information, or manipulate attendance records. (2) Any required proof of attendance or absence must be authentic, readable, accurate, and submitted within the applicable deadline. Submission does not guarantee automatic approval.

6. Verification of Submitted Proofs

Authorized Representatives and Administrators are responsible for reviewing submitted proofs of attendance or absence. Submissions may be approved or rejected based on their authenticity, completeness, relevance, and compliance with program requirements. Users must provide additional information when reasonably required and must not interfere with or improperly influence the verification process.

7. Activity Management

Administrators are responsible for creating, updating, and managing scholarship activities in the system. Activity names, schedules, venues, descriptions, and participation requirements should be accurate and updated when necessary. Iskolars and Representatives must follow the published activity guidelines and official announcements.

8. Announcements and Notifications

Authorized Administrators and Representatives may publish announcements regarding scholarship activities, schedules, requirements, and other relevant information. Users should review announcements and notifications regularly. Posting or distributing false, misleading, inappropriate, or unauthorized information through system features is prohibited.

9. User Management and Access Privileges

Administrators manage user accounts, update information when authorized, and assign appropriate roles and permissions. Representatives must only perform actions within their assigned responsibilities. Users must not impersonate another person, create unauthorized accounts, or attempt to obtain privileges beyond their assigned roles.

10. Reports and Record Export

Authorized personnel may generate, view, and export attendance reports for legitimate scholarship administration, monitoring, evaluation, and reporting. Exported reports must not be falsified, manipulated, or distributed to unauthorized individuals. Personal information contained in reports must remain confidential.

11. Privacy and Data Confidentiality

All users must respect the privacy and confidentiality of personal information stored in BASIS, including profile details, attendance records, scorecards, and other scholarship-related information. Personal information must only be accessed, processed, and shared for authorized purposes. Users must not disclose, copy, distribute, or misuse another person's information without proper authorization. The collection, use, storage, and processing of personal information must comply with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

12. Feedback, Concerns, and User Support

Users may report technical issues, incorrect attendance records, scorecard discrepancies, or other system-related concerns through the designated communication channels. All feedback must be accurate, respectful, and relevant to the Iskolar ng Bagac Program. Users should provide sufficient details to help authorized personnel investigate and resolve reported concerns.

13. System Security and Proper Use

BASIS must only be used for its intended scholarship and administrative purposes. Users must not attempt to hack, damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to the system or its database. Uploading malicious files, exploiting system vulnerabilities, interfering with attendance monitoring, or intentionally disrupting system functions is prohibited. Users should log out after using shared or public computers.

BASIS may occasionally experience downtime, maintenance, technical errors, or temporary interruptions. Reasonable efforts should be made to maintain system functionality and address reported problems. Users should report technical issues through the designated support channels. When technical problems prevent access to records, users should contact an authorized Representative or Administrator for guidance.

15. Violations and Account Restrictions

Violations of these Terms and Conditions may result in a warning, temporary account restriction, suspension of access, or referral to the appropriate program authorities, depending on the nature and severity of the violation. Actions taken must be consistent with applicable program policies and relevant laws. 16. Updates and Changes to Terms

These Terms and Conditions may be revised to reflect changes in system features, scholarship procedures, security requirements, or applicable laws.
