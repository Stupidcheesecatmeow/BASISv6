WELCOME TO BASIS MABUHAY, ISKOLAR!

WELCOME TO BASIS!

Welcome to BASIS (Bagac Attendance and Scholar Information System)! We're happy to have you as part of the Iskolar ng Bagac community. This platform is designed to make your scholarship journey easier and more organized by helping you manage your personal information, monitor attendance, track activities, and stay updated on important announcements. We hope BASIS helps you grow and achieve your dreams as an Iskolar ng Bagac!

Being an Iskolar ng Bagac is an opportunity to learn, grow, and work toward a brighter future. We hope BASIS will be a helpful companion throughout your journey as you continue pursuing your dreams and making your community proud.

Before proceeding, please take a moment to read the guidelines on the next page to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

Once again, welcome to BASIS, Iskolar! We're glad you're here!

GUIDELINES!

1. Complete your profile. Fill in all required personal and scholar information and make sure your details are accurate and updated.

2. Check announcements. Regularly check for new announcements, important reminders, and scholarship-related updates.

3. View activities. Check ongoing activities and their requirements to ensure compliance with your responsibilities as a scholar.

4. Monitor your scorecard. Regularly check your scorecard to track your attendance and monitor your progress.

5. Send feedback or concerns. Go to the More section and select Feedback and Concerns if you encounter any problems or issues.

Remember: Stay active, keep your information updated, and check BASIS regularly to make the most of your experience as an Iskolar ng Bagac!

ACTIVITY ATTENDANCE AND COMPLIANCE

Every Activity Matters!

Participating in activities is an important responsibility of every Iskolar ng Bagac. Always bring your own QR code and make sure it is scanned twice for your time-in and time-out to ensure that your attendance is properly recorded.

If you cannot attend an activity, submit a letter of absence through the Submission Bin as soon as possible. If QR code scanning is required, coordinate with your assigned proxy, make sure they bring your QR code, and know who will represent you.

For activities that allow proxies, you may ask a trusted relative or friend to represent you, provided that they are not a fellow Iskolar ng Bagac .

Always stay informed about activity requirements and remember that your participation and compliance matter!
