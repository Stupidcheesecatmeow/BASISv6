WELCOME TO BASIS!

Welcome to BASIS (Bagac Attendance and Scholar Information System)! This system helps make your Iskolar ng Bagac experience easier by allowing you to manage your information, monitor attendance, track activities, and stay updated.

We’re glad to have you as part of the Iskolar ng Bagac community!

Please check the guidelines on the next page before proceeding.

GUIDELINES!

1. Complete your profile first. Make sure that all required personal and scholar information is properly filled out before proceeding.

2. Submit your registration. Review your information carefully and submit your registration once all required details are complete and accurate.

3. Wait for confirmation. After submitting your registration, please wait for the administrator to review and confirm your account.

4. Access the full site. Once your registration has been confirmed, you will have full access to the features and services available on the website.
